How To Play

Navigate platforms and dangerous traps to reach the spatial gateway.


Mechanic: Elements match specific light spectrum channels. Changing your light configuration toggles physical reality. Matches pass solids, mismatches block your path.


Controls:
Move: A/D or Left/Right Arrow Keys
Jump: W, Space, or Up Arrow Key
Spectrum Keys: Numbers 1 through 7 match the top display channels.
R: Instantly restart your immediate level matrix.
